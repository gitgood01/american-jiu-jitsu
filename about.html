What makes it American?

American Jiu Jitsu originates from the work of pioneers including John Danaher and his students, Gordon Ryan and Craig Jones. It also pulls fromt he work of other accomplished Americans, such as catch wrestlers like Josh Barnett.


Fundamentally there are differences as well. AJJ focuses on a powerful standing game with limited inversions and leg entrapments to allow for a better transition to MMA, while including different submissions and discarding the Gi for street clothes.
